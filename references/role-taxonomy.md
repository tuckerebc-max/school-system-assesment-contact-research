# Role Taxonomy

## Tier 1 - required for every district

### Superintendent / Chief Executive

Possible titles include Superintendent, Chancellor, Chief Executive Officer, President and CEO,
Head of Schools, Executive Director, Interim Superintendent, and Acting Superintendent.

### Chief Academic Officer / Teaching and Learning

Possible titles include Chief Academic Officer, Deputy Superintendent for Academics, Teaching
and Learning, or Instruction; Associate Superintendent for Academics; Assistant Superintendent
for Curriculum and Instruction; Chief of Curriculum and Instruction; Chief Learning Officer;
Executive Director of Academics; and Director of Curriculum and Instruction.

### Assessment, Accountability, Research, and Evaluation

Possible titles include Chief Accountability Officer; Chief of Assessment and Accountability;
Assistant Superintendent for Assessment and Accountability; Executive Director or Director of
Assessment, Accountability, Testing, Research, Evaluation, Performance Management, or Data and
Accountability.

### Student / Scholar Services

Possible titles include Chief Student Services Officer; Chief of Student Support; Chief of Schools
and Student Services; Deputy or Assistant Superintendent for Student Services; Executive Director
of Student Services; Director of Student Support Services; Chief of Whole Child Supports, Student
Wellness, Pupil Services, or Scholar Services.

Interpret this as broad responsibility for student support, success, attendance, behavior,
wellness, counseling, or whole-child services. Do not automatically equate it with special
education.

## Tier 2 - research systematically

### Special Education / Exceptional Student Services

Possible titles include Chief, Assistant Superintendent, Executive Director, or Director of
Special Education, Exceptional Student Education, Exceptional Children, or combined Student
Support and Special Education.

### Technology, Data, and Innovation

Possible titles include Chief Technology Officer, Chief Information Officer, Chief Innovation
Officer, Chief Data Officer, Deputy Superintendent for Technology, Executive Director of
Technology, Director of Educational Technology, and Director of Information Systems.

### Communications / Community Engagement

Possible titles include Chief Communications Officer, Chief External Affairs Officer, Chief
Engagement Officer, Executive Director or Director of Communications, Public Information Officer,
Director of Community Engagement, and Director of Family and Community Engagement.

## Tier 3 - include when clearly present

### School Leadership / School Support

Include a senior systemwide leader responsible for supervising, supporting, or managing schools
or networks of schools. Do not use this category merely because a deputy superintendent is senior;
the source must show substantive school-leadership or school-support responsibility.

## Mapping rules

- Use standardized categories exactly as written above.
- Preserve the source's exact current title separately.
- Use one record per district-role-leader combination.
- Create multiple standardized-role records when one leader substantively owns multiple functions.
- Include multiple leaders only when portfolios are distinct and equally relevant.
- Explain non-obvious mappings and distributed functions in Research Notes.
